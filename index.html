UK Road Legal Compliance Standards

Are 3D Gel and 4D Laser Cut acrylic plates completely road legal?

Yes, fully. Under active British regulations, raised dimensional solid black fonts are 100% legal for highway deployment across the UK, provided the layout accurately preserves the strict spacing rules and uses authorized Charles Wright lettering dimensions.

What structural upgrades define the new BS AU 145e standard?

The updated BS AU 145e specification establishes heightened resilience guidelines. Materials undergo rigorous mechanical validation testing against stones, extreme temperature weathering cycles, near-surface friction limits, and camera readability validation constraints.

Which baseline verification documents do I need to supply?

To safely manufacture legal registration displays, we require validation verifying the coordinator's identity (such as a valid UK driving licence or passport check) alongside vehicle entitlement confirmation (such as a V5C logbook form or V750 retention document).
